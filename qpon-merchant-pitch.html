The Reality for Local Businesses

You're Working Hard.
The Middlemen Are Getting Paid.

💸

Commissions Eat Your Margin

OTAs and delivery apps charge 20–30% on every transaction. For a café doing LKR 500K/month, that's LKR 100–150K gone before you pay staff.

Up to 30% lost per sale
👥

No Direct Customer Relationship

Third-party platforms own the customer data. You fulfil the order — they keep the contact, the reviews, and the repeat business opportunity.

Zero ownership of your audience
📉

Empty Tables in Off-Peak Hours

Weekday lunches, mid-afternoon slots, low-season months — capacity sits idle. There's no smart tool to attract customers precisely when you need them.

~70% of off-peak capacity wasted
🚫
No visibility without ads
Running Meta/Google ads costs LKR 50–200K/mo with unpredictable ROI for most SMEs.

No real analytics
Merchants have no clear view of which promotions work, when, and for which customer type.
🔄
